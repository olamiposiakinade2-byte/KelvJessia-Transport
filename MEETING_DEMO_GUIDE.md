# KelvJessia Transport — five-minute meeting demonstration

## Minute 1 — Homepage and concept

Open the homepage and point out the clear demonstration banner, the headline, and the two primary actions: **Find a Trip** and **How It Works**. Explain that the design is ready for a production booking platform but currently uses safe sample data.

## Minute 2 — Trip search and seats

Choose a sample pickup, destination, date, departure time, and passenger count. Show the resulting sample schedules, remaining simulated seats, and the validation that prevents a request exceeding sample capacity.

## Minute 3 — Booking and WhatsApp handoff

Select a sample trip, enter fictional details, and show the review screen. Use **Edit details** once, then choose **Continue to WhatsApp**. Explain that WhatsApp opens a prepared request, but the customer must tap Send and the request stays awaiting company confirmation.

## Minute 4 — Admin demonstration dashboard

Open `/#admin` or select **Admin demo** in the footer. Highlight the **DEMO MODE** badge, summary cards, search, date, destination, and status filters. Confirm or cancel a sample record and return to the same trip search to show its simulated availability changes.

## Minute 5 — Reporting and business value

Apply a filter, then export CSV and download the PDF report. Explain that reports include only sample records in the current browser. Close by describing the future production path: central database, approved administration, real availability, and audited customer booking records.

## Demonstration boundaries

- Working demonstration: responsive UI, sample seat calculations, booking validation, review screen, WhatsApp handoff, local sample dashboard, PDF, and CSV.
- Future production work: secure staff accounts, centralized bookings, real schedules/prices, real-time inventory, automatic notifications, and approved payments.
